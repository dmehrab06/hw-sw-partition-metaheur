\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This paper positions \diffgnnorder as the main method for scheduling-aware HW/SW partitioning and uses the placement-only \diffgnn model strictly as an ablation. The manuscript now aligns the notation, algorithm description, and evaluation protocol with the ICCAD-style presentation: every method is compared against the DAG baseline, non-order-aware methods are reported by static-priority makespan, and \diffgnnorder is reported by the better of its static and learned-priority schedules.

The initial pilot experiments already show the main effect that motivates the method. On the Small-11 graph, the ordering head improves the reported makespan from $31$ to $29$, matching the best pilot metaheuristic results. On the larger SqueezeNet-TOSA and Synthetic-50 pilots, the results are more mixed: \diffgnnorder remains competitive, but the learned ordering does not yet consistently outperform the strongest static baselines under the reduced-budget draft runs. The final version of the paper will therefore complete the full multi-seed evaluation, populate the remaining ablation rows for post-processing, refinement, and fixed edge weights, and report the definitive large-scale synthetic results at 1,000 and 10,000 nodes.
